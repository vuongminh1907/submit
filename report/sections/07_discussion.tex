\section{Discussion and limitations}

The weakest two-period results are setA-16 and setA-19, with mean gaps $0.67$ and $0.56$
after one hour.
The best feasible value among the ten runs is better than the reported mean: $0.1025$ for
setA-16 and $0.0796$ for setA-19.
A missing MLU certificate stays ``Not cert.'', and a bound that was not computed is never
written as zero.

Two modelling choices delimit the scope of the tables.
First, the formulation optimizes~\eqref{eq:mlu}, not the full lexicographic vector of
loads~\citep{orange2026}; two routings with the same peak may still differ on the remaining
sorted loads.
Second, the split coefficients follow the course note~\citep{courser2026} rather than a
router-local ECMP recursion.
Within those choices, every row comes from the MIP above, either compact or as a path
formulation with a computed lower bound on~$L$.
